# Security Specification & Payload-First TDD (`Veridian Ledger`)

## 1. Core Data Invariants
1. **Strict Ownership Isolation**: Every private financial record (`bank_accounts`, `brokerage_accounts`, `transactions`, `budgets`, `batch_jobs`, `payments`, `financial_reports`, `dtc_registrations`, `brokerage_firms`, `tax_documents`, `asset_ingestions`, `staged_assets`, `assets`, `bank_tx_architectures`, `brokerage_tx_architectures`, `rail_architectures`, `acquisition_architectures`, `company_accounts`) must belong to the authenticated, email-verified user (`ownerId == request.auth.uid`). Cross-user reads, queries, and mutations are unconditionally denied.
2. **PII Split Isolation & Zero-PII Public Accounts**: User PII (`email`, `phone`, `legalAddress`, `taxIdLast4`) and Account Recovery secrets/contacts (`recoveryEmail`, `recoveryPhone`, `trustedContactName`, `trustedContactEmail`, `hardwareKeyLabel`) are isolated under `/users/{userId}/private/profile` and `/users/{userId}/private/recovery` and are never listable or readable by any non-owner. `public_accounts` contain zero PII and only permit non-owner reads when `visibility == 'public_directory' && status == 'published'`.
3. **Relational & Atomic Integrity**: A `UserWorkspace` (`purchasesAccountId`), `BrokerageAccount` (`firmId`, `linkedBankAccountId`), `Transaction`, `Payment`, `FinancialReport`, `TaxDocument`, `StagedAsset`, `OnboardedAsset` (`accountId`, `firmId`, `brokerageAccountId`), `BankTxArchitecture`, `BrokerageTxArchitecture`, `RailArchitecture`, `AcquisitionArchitecture` (`fundingAccountId`, `firmId`, `escrowPaymentId`, `onboardedAssetId`), `CompanyAccount`, or `PublicAccount` cannot reference a non-existent `BankAccount`, `BrokerageAccount`, `BrokerageFirm`, `AssetIngestion`, `Payment`, or `OnboardedAsset`, nor a document owned by a different user (`existsAfter` + `getAfter` validation).
4. **Terminal State & Field-Verification Locking**:
   - `Payment` records in `settled` or `cancelled` status are immutable.
   - `BatchJob` records in `committed` status are immutable.
   - `DtcRegistration` records in `approved` status are immutable.
   - `TaxDocument` records in `filed` status are immutable.
   - `AssetIngestion` records in `committed` status are immutable and support bounded immutable `rawDocumentText` (<= 8,000 chars); `StagedAsset` records must start in `pending_review`, support bounded immutable `sourceExcerpt` (<= 800 chars), require `nameVerified && identifierVerified && valuationVerified` before reaching `verified` or `committed`, require an atomic `assets/{committedAssetId}` creation on commit, and are immutable once `committed`; `OnboardedAsset` records in `archived` status are immutable.
   - `OnboardedAsset` records cannot be created directly in `custodied` status without passing MFA verification (`custodyStatus == 'pending_verification' && mfaVerified == false` on create).
   - `BrokerageAccount` records in `closed` status are immutable; `cashBalance` and `marginDebt` must be finite non-negative numbers.
   - `AcquisitionArchitecture` records must start in `pipeline` or `diligence` stage, enforce atomic escrow payment + asset onboarding linkage in workflow steps, and are immutable once `closed` or `cancelled`.
   - `CompanyAccount` records in `dissolved` status are immutable; `PublicAccount` records in `archived` status are immutable.
5. **Temporal Integrity**: All `createdAt` and `updatedAt` timestamps must equal `request.time`; `createdAt` and `ownerId` are immutable after creation.

## 2. The "Dirty Dozen" Adversarial Payloads
1. **Identity Spoofing on Create**: `transactions/tx_1`, `brokerage_accounts/brk_1`, `staged_assets/stg_1`, `assets/ast_1`, `acquisition_architectures/acq_1`, `company_accounts/co_1`, or `public_accounts/pub_1` with `ownerId: "victim_uid"` while authenticated as `"attacker_uid"`. -> `PERMISSION_DENIED`.
2. **Shadow Field Injection**: `bank_accounts/acc_1`, `brokerage_accounts/brk_1`, `staged_assets/stg_1`, `acquisition_architectures/acq_1`, `company_accounts/co_1`, or `public_accounts/pub_1` containing all required fields plus ghost field `isAdminApproved: true`. -> `PERMISSION_DENIED`.
3. **Unverified Email Bypass**: Write attempt when `request.auth.token.email_verified == false`. -> `PERMISSION_DENIED`.
4. **Orphaned Transaction / Account / Ingestion Reference Injection**: `transactions/tx_2`, `brokerage_accounts/brk_2` referencing `linkedBankAccountId: "nonexistent_acc"`, `staged_assets/stg_2` referencing `ingestionId: "nonexistent_ing"`, `acquisition_architectures/acq_2` referencing `fundingAccountId: "nonexistent_acc"`, `company_accounts/co_2`, or `public_accounts/pub_2` referencing `accountId`/`linkedAccountId: "nonexistent_acc"`. -> `PERMISSION_DENIED`.
5. **Cross-Tenant Account Reference**: `payments/pay_1`, `brokerage_accounts/brk_3`, `staged_assets/stg_3`, `acquisition_architectures/acq_3`, `company_accounts/co_3`, or `public_accounts/pub_3` with `ownerId: attacker_uid` referencing `sourceAccountId`/`linkedBankAccountId`/`ingestionId`/`fundingAccountId`/`linkedAccountId` owned by `victim_uid`. -> `PERMISSION_DENIED`.
6. **Terminal State Re-opening**: Updating `payments/pay_settled` from `status: "settled"` to `"pending_mfa"`, `brokerage_accounts/brk_closed` from `status: "closed"` to `"active"`, `staged_assets/stg_com` from `reviewStatus: "committed"` to `"pending_review"`, `acquisition_architectures/acq_closed` from `stage: "closed"` to `"pipeline"`, `company_accounts/co_dis` from `status: "dissolved"` to `"active"`, or `public_accounts/pub_arch` from `status: "archived"` to `"published"`. -> `PERMISSION_DENIED`.
7. **State Shortcutting on DTC, Staging Review, Acquisition & Asset Custody**: Creating `dtc_registrations/dtc_1` directly with `status: "approved"`, creating `staged_assets/stg_4` directly with `reviewStatus: "verified"`, creating `acquisition_architectures/acq_4` directly with `stage: "closed"`, committing `staged_assets/stg_4` when `nameVerified == false`, or creating `assets/ast_4` directly with `custodyStatus: "custodied"` without MFA verification. -> `PERMISSION_DENIED`.
8. **ID Poisoning (Denial of Wallet)**: Creating `/transactions/`, `/brokerage_accounts/`, `/acquisition_architectures/`, `/company_accounts/`, or `/public_accounts/` with a 500-character document ID containing special characters `../../bad$id`. -> `PERMISSION_DENIED`.
9. **Client Timestamp Forgery**: Creating a transaction, brokerage account, acquisition architecture, company account, or public account with a backdated `createdAt` instead of `request.time`. -> `PERMISSION_DENIED`.
10. **Array Overflow on Brokerage Regulations**: Creating `brokerage_firms/firm_1` with 25 entries in `regulations` or non-string array element `regulations: [123]`. -> `PERMISSION_DENIED`.
11. **Value Poisoning on Update**: Updating `budgets/bud_1` `monthlyLimit` to `-500`, `brokerage_accounts/brk_1` `cashBalance` or `marginDebt` to `-100`, `acquisition_architectures/acq_1` `escrowHoldbackPct` to `85`, `company_accounts/co_1` `authorizedSignersCount` to `0`, or `public_accounts/pub_1` `disclosedReserveUsd` to `-1`. -> `PERMISSION_DENIED`.
12. **Blanket List Scraping**: Executing `list` query on `/transactions`, `/brokerage_accounts`, `/acquisition_architectures`, `/company_accounts`, or unlisted `/public_accounts` without `where("ownerId", "==", uid)` or attempting to list `/users`. -> `PERMISSION_DENIED`.
